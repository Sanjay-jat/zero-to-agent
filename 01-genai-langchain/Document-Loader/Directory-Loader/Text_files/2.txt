Cricket is a bat-and-ball sport played between two teams of eleven players. It is one of the most popular sports in the world and is especially loved in countries such as India, Australia, England, Pakistan, and South Africa. The game is played on a large field with a 22-yard pitch at its center. Players score runs by hitting the ball and running between the wickets, while the fielding team tries to dismiss the batters. Cricket has three major formats: Test, One Day International (ODI), and Twenty20 (T20). The sport teaches teamwork, patience, discipline, strategy, and sportsmanship, making it a favorite among millions of fans worldwide.

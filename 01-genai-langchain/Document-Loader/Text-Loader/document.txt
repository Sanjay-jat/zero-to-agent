Virat Kohli is one of the greatest cricketers of the modern era and has inspired millions of fans with his dedication, discipline, and consistency. Born on November 5, 1988, in Delhi, India, he made his international debut in 2008 and quickly established himself as one of the world's best batsmen. Known for his aggressive batting style, exceptional fitness, and ability to perform under pressure, Kohli has scored thousands of runs across Test, One Day International (ODI), and Twenty20 International (T20I) formats. He has captained the Indian cricket team in all three formats and has led the team to numerous memorable victories. Apart from his cricketing achievements, Virat Kohli is admired for his passion, hard work, and commitment to excellence, making him a role model for aspiring athletes around the world.
